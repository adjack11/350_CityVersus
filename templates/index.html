CityVS was created to take the guesswork out of relocating by offering a clean, transparent way to compare cities based on the metrics that matter most. Built as part of a software engineering project (CSE-350) at the University of Louisville, the platform cuts through the noise by delivering key data using official, up-to-date government sources without visual clutter. While the site currently focuses on the 10 major cities closest to our region, it gives users an effortless, distraction-free tool to evaluate their options side by side.
